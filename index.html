Smart, not just fast

A correctness battery for local LLMs on Apple Silicon — built by a learner, shared for feedback.

I'm new to this and still learning. But something bothered me about how we evaluate local models: almost all the published numbers are speed (tokens/sec). Speed tells you how fast a model talks — not whether what it says is correct. A fast, confident, wrong answer is not a helpful assistant.

My goal is a local assistant for ~90% of my personal work (finance, coding, analysis), keeping frontier models for heavy-duty coding. For that I want smart, not merely fastest. So I built a small battery to measure correctness, and ran six configurations through it on two M5 Ultra Macs (256 GB each, plus a two-Mac tandem).

What we found

  • Faster did not mean smarter. On the same model weights, the faster serving engine (TensorFold) scored lower on correctness than oMLX — for both Qwen3.8-Flash-Next and GLM-5.3-Flash. Speed and reliability traded off.
  • The two-Mac setup didn't beat a single Mac. GLM-6-bit over Tandem (two Macs) ranked below every single-Mac oMLX configuration on this battery.
  • Honesty is probe-dependent. No model passed every calibration trap. The best behavior was acknowledging when the facts don't determine an answer, then giving a clearly-flagged best guess — not confident fabrication.
  • The strongest overall was Qwen3.8-Flash-Next 6-bit on oMLX; the 4-bit version nearly matched it.
These are observations on this battery and these configurations — not a claim that any engine or model is universally better. Templates, sampling and reasoning settings differ between engines, so a case-level difference doesn't isolate a single cause. TensorFold's speed is real and verified; the point is simply that speed alone didn't predict correctness here.

The battery — 15 questions, 5 areas

Finance (multi-step math), code correctness, constraint logic, honesty/calibration, and long-context integration. Full prompts and gold answers are in CASES-v2.json. Suggestions welcome — I'd love a better battery than mine.

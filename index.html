Results withdrawn pending grading audit

Correction notice — November 25, 2026

The previously published scores, rankings and comparative conclusions are not validated and should not be cited. We removed the tables while auditing the original saved answers. No replacement ranking is available yet.

What happened

Our automatic grader incorrectly rejected a calibrated conditional answer, a valid alternative debugging example, and correct negative-number notation for losses. We are rechecking every configuration for both false penalties and undeserved credit.

Truncation and infrastructure failures will be distinguished from wrong reasoning. Ambiguous questions and rubric changes will be documented rather than silently changing the meaning of the test.

What we are withdrawing

The earlier claims that oMLX is more correct than TensorFold, that one model is the overall winner, and that two-Mac operation does not earn its complexity are withdrawn. The experiment did not isolate engine causality or matched hardware scaling, and the scoring needs correction.

Questions remain open for feedback

The draft question file remains available, but its gold answers and rubrics are under review. Some public prompts were edited after testing; they are not the exact historical test version. The document exercises are short multi-fact tasks, not validated large-context stress tests.

The original grader is experimental and executes model-provided Python; do not use it with untrusted responses without isolation.

See the repository README for the correction and coverage details, or results.json for machine-readable withdrawal status.

Original evidence is preserved. Verified revised results will include a change log. Suggestions for better questions and evaluation methods are welcome.

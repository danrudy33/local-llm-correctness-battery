A leading group, not a decisive intelligence winner

Qwen TFQ3 is our provisional practical candidate: tied highest primary score and about 79 GiB checkpoint storage. GLM TFQ3 ties on primary score but was slower here. Qwen6 oMLX also ties if the conventional unstated liquidity cap is accepted. Under strict missing-cap treatment GLM TFQ3 becomes 91.7%; Qwen TFQ3 stays 93.3%.

Limitations matter

Every configuration exhausted the mortgage output budget without a final answer. The supplied-policy question is ambiguous as an honesty test. The document tasks are short, not long-context stress tests. Code received static semantic review in this audit. Runtime settings differ, so neither engine causality nor matched hardware scaling was established.

Twenty-one historical scores changed. Original evidence is preserved; two purchase-derived original prompt/answer texts are withheld from public evidence for privacy. Edited public draft variants are unscored and are not the historical test version.
